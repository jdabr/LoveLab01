3. How LoveLab works

- Responses are generated by an artificial intelligence model provided by OpenAI. AI responses can be wrong, incomplete or inappropriate for your situation. Use your own judgment and do not rely on LoveLab for important decisions.
- LoveLab only discusses relationships and interpersonal dynamics. Messages on other topics may be declined. If you mention that you or someone else may be in danger, LoveLab is designed to respond and share the crisis resources listed in Section 1. Because it relies on AI and online services, it may not always do so, so never rely on LoveLab in an emergency.
- LoveLab replies in English.
- Messages are limited to 1,000 characters. To keep the service available for everyone, the number of messages you can send in a given period is limited.
- You can keep several chats and group them by the people they are about. The names and relationships you add are saved only in your browser on your device. They are never sent to us or to OpenAI, and LoveLab is not told who a chat is about. Anything you type in a message, including names, is sent as described in Section 5.
- Your chats stay available if you refresh the page, and are cleared when you close the tab. If you turn on "Remember my chats on this device", they are instead kept in your browser on that device, so you can return to them after closing your browser or restarting your device. Once 30 days have passed since a chat's last message, it is deleted automatically the next time you open LoveLab. A person you added is deleted the same way once they have no chats and have not been used for 30 days. If you turn the setting off, everything is cleared when you close the tab. Anyone who uses the same browser could see your chats, so do not turn this on for a shared device.
- Deleting a chat (or a person, which deletes all of their chats) erases it from your device straight away, along with LoveLab's memory of it. LoveLab's servers do not keep your chat history. This does not affect the anonymized copies we keep, or the copies OpenAI may keep for a limited time, as described in Section 5.
- LoveLab has no accounts, so chats cannot be moved to another device or browser.

5. Privacy: what we collect and how it is used

LoveLab is designed to be anonymous. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

What we store. Each time LoveLab replies to a message, we store:
- your message (up to the first 255 characters) and LoveLab's response (up to the first 3,000 characters);
- the date and time;
- an anonymous visitor ID; and
- technical details about the reply: whether it answered or declined your message, how long it took, how much processing it used, and whether it included crisis resources.
If a reply fails, or you leave before it finishes, we store only the date and time, the anonymous visitor ID and these technical details, not your message. We also keep daily totals of these details (for example, how many messages were answered each day), which contain no messages or visitor IDs.
Before anything is stored, email addresses, phone numbers and other long numbers, links and social media handles are automatically replaced with placeholders such as [email] or [number].

What we do not store. We do not store your IP address with your messages or anywhere in our database. It is used to create the anonymous visitor ID and to enforce message limits. For message limits, it is held in a temporary store for up to 24 hours after your last message and then deleted automatically. The visitor ID is generated using a secret that changes every day. It lets us see which messages were sent by the same visitor on the same day. Shortly after each day ends (midnight UTC), that day's secret is permanently deleted, and the ID can no longer be linked to your IP address, to you, or to your visits on other days.

How it is used. We use stored conversations to operate LoveLab, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse and improve the service.

Who it is shared with.
- OpenAI: your message and the recent messages in that chat are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. Our server sends them, so OpenAI does not receive your IP address. OpenAI processes this data under its own API terms and policies (https://openai.com/policies/). Under those policies, OpenAI may keep API data for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, and by default it does not use API data to train its models.
- Hosting providers: LoveLab and its database run on Heroku, a cloud hosting provider. As part of operating the service, Heroku may briefly record technical information such as IP addresses in its own system logs.
- We may disclose information if required by law.
We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage. LoveLab does not use cookies. It saves the following in your browser's storage on your device: your light/dark mode preference; the fact that you agreed to these Terms (so you are not asked again until they change); whether "Remember my chats on this device" is turned on; and your chats and the people you have added (as described in Section 3). Each chat also holds a copy of its recent messages, digitally signed by us so it can't be altered without us noticing, which your browser sends back with your next message in that chat so LoveLab can reply in context. Apart from that copy, this browser storage stays on your device and is not sent to us. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it. Stored conversations are currently kept without a fixed deletion date. We may delete them at any time.

Access and deletion requests. Because stored conversations are anonymous, we cannot tell which conversations belong to you, so we are unable to locate, provide or delete a specific person's conversations. Please do not include information that could identify you or others, such as full names, addresses or account details. Automatic filtering catches common contact details but cannot remove everything.
