A calm place to think through the relationships in your life

LoveLab is an AI companion for questions about partners, family, friends and coworkers. Talk through what's happening, see it from another angle, and work out what to say next. No account, and no judgment.

Start a chat

How it works

  1. Say what's on your mind

    Describe what's going on in your own words. The more detail you share, the more useful LoveLab's guidance can be.

  2. Keep chats about each person together

    Add the people in your life, like a partner, a parent or a coworker, and your chats about each of them stay grouped in one place on your device.

  3. Get warm, practical guidance

    LoveLab listens, reflects back what it hears, and suggests practical next steps, often with a question to help you think it through.

Things you could ask

  • "My partner and I keep having the same argument. How do we break the cycle?"
  • "How do I set boundaries with my mom without hurting her?"
  • "A friend hasn't replied in weeks. Should I reach out?"
  • "My coworker keeps taking credit for my work. How do I bring it up?"

What LoveLab isn't

  • It's an AI, not a person. No human reads replies before you do.
  • It's not therapy, or medical, legal or professional advice.
  • It can be wrong. Use your own judgment for important decisions.
  • It only discusses relationships, and always replies in English.

If you or someone else is in danger

LoveLab isn't an emergency service. Call 911 (or your local emergency number). In the U.S., call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or reach the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (or text START to 88788).

Privacy and safety

No account, no names

LoveLab never asks who you are. There's nothing to sign up for.

Your chats live on your device

Chats are saved in your browser and cleared when you close the tab, unless you turn on Remember my chats, which keeps them for 30 days. LoveLab's servers don't keep your chat history.

People stay private

The names and relationships you add never leave your device. LoveLab isn't told who a chat is about.

Delete anytime

Deleting a chat erases it from your device and wipes LoveLab's memory of it straight away.

Anonymous records

To run and improve LoveLab, an anonymized copy of each message is kept, with emails, phone numbers and links removed, no IP address, and no way to link it to you.

Safety comes first

Messages about danger, abuse or self-harm always get a response, along with crisis resources.

Frequently asked questions

Am I talking to a real person?

No. Replies are written by an AI model provided by OpenAI, and no human reviews them before you see them.

Can I come back to a chat later?

Yes, on the same device and browser. Chats stay while the tab is open, even if you refresh. Turn on Remember my chats on this device to keep them after closing your browser, for 30 days after each chat's last message. There are no accounts, so chats can't move to another device.

Who can see my messages?

Your messages are sent to OpenAI to write each reply. LoveLab's server sends them, so OpenAI doesn't get your IP address. LoveLab keeps anonymized copies (described above), which can't be traced back to you. If you turn on Remember my chats, anyone who uses your browser could open them, so leave it off on shared devices.

Does LoveLab know who the people in my chats are?

No. The names and relationships you add are only used to organize your chats on your device. If you mention someone's name in a message, it's sent along with the rest of that message, so you may prefer initials or a nickname.

Why did LoveLab decline my question?

LoveLab only discusses relationships and the people in your life. If your question was about something else, try asking how it affects someone you're close to.

How do I delete everything?

Delete chats one at a time, or delete a person to remove all their chats at once. Turning off Remember my chats means everything is cleared when you close the tab. Clearing your browser's data for this site also removes it all.
